Our Commitment to Your Safety

Security & Privacy

CyberShield is built around a simple principle: your data stays under your control. Below we explain exactly how your files, backups, passwords, and sessions are handled — and what we intentionally never do.

File Security

How uploaded files are handled & protected

How Uploaded Files Are Handled

Files you upload are processed immediately in memory and never stored longer than necessary. Each file is read, hashed, and encrypted on-the-fly.

How Files Are Protected

Files that are backed up are encrypted using Fernet (AES-128) before being written to disk. A SHA-256 hash verifies integrity end-to-end.

Temporary Files Are Deleted Automatically

After processing, the temporary uploaded copy is automatically removed from the server to protect your privacy and reduce unnecessary storage.

Encrypted Backups Persist Until You Delete Them

Your encrypted backup remains available for recovery until you choose to delete it. We never remove your backups automatically.

Privacy Policy

What we store — and what we never store

What Information Is Stored

We store only what is required to run the service: your account credentials (fully hashed), module usage statistics, backup metadata, and records you explicitly create.

What Information Is NOT Stored

We do not sell or share personal data, do not store original uploaded files, do not store plain-text passwords, and do not track your browsing beyond what is required for security.

Privacy Commitments

We commit to collecting minimal data, encrypting sensitive information, allowing you to delete your own data, being transparent about security practices, and following recognized security frameworks such as OWASP.

Data Protection

Defense-in-depth for everything you store with us

Minimal Data Collection

Only the data needed to provide the service is requested.

Encryption at Rest

Sensitive stored data is encrypted rather than kept in plain text.

Access Control

Data is isolated per user; you only access your own records.

Secure Authentication

How your passwords are stored & protected

Passwords Are Securely Hashed

Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are transformed using a strong, salted one-way hashing algorithm so they cannot be recovered or guessed from the database.

Protection Against Common Attacks

Login flows are protected against brute-force attempts, include CAPTCHA verification, and use secure session handling to prevent session hijacking.

Backup & Recovery

How backups are created and kept safe

How Backups Are Created

When you use the Ransomware / Backup module, your uploaded file is securely encrypted and stored in your personal backup folder. A SHA-256 integrity hash is recorded so the backup can be verified later.

How Encrypted Backups Remain Available

Your encrypted backups stay protected on the server until you decide to delete them. Deletion is always a manual, explicit user action — never automatic.

Session Security

How your sessions are protected

Secure Cookie Settings

Session cookies are marked HttpOnly to block JavaScript access, use SameSite=Lax to prevent cross-site request forging, and are configured with a limited lifetime.

Session Expiry

Sessions automatically expire after a period of inactivity, reducing the window in which a stolen session could be misused.
